Advanced AI Detection Technology

Our system utilizes YOLO (You Only Look Once), the latest state-of-the-art object detection model renowned for its speed and accuracy. Specifically trained to identify four critical weapon types: Gun, Handgun, Knife, and Grenade.

The model achieves remarkable precision with minimal false positives, making it ideal for security applications where accuracy and reliability are paramount.
